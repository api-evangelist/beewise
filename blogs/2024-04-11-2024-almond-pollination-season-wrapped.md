Almond pollination season in California is arguably the largest single agricultural event in the world. Each year, more than 2.5 million bee colonies arrive from all over the US, all at the same time, as growers work to ensure that their orchards receive adequate pollination for a successful crop yield. Almonds & Bees With no bees, we would have no almonds.
