Honey bees are important pollinators of many of the berries we enjoy eating. We all know berries are packed with natural sugars, making them a sweet treat for us, but what you may not know is that berry blossoms are also dense with highly nutritious nectar, making them important forage for bees. This relationship between berries and bees is mutually beneficial.
